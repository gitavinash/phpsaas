# Facebook Lead Ads & Meta Webhooks Integration

## Overview
Connect Facebook and Instagram advertising campaigns to automatically capture traveler lead submissions, sync custom form fields into the CRM, and attribute leads to specific campaigns and ad sets.

## Technical Specifications
- **Plugin Identifier**: `facebook`
- **Category**: Lead Generation & Advertising
- **Webhook Endpoint**: `https://yourdomain.com/webhook/facebook/`
- **Required Meta Graph Permissions**:
  - `pages_show_list`
  - `pages_read_engagement`
  - `leads_retrieval`
  - `pages_manage_ads`

## Features
- **Real-Time Webhook Synchronization**: Leads captured on Facebook Forms arrive in your CRM within milliseconds.
- **Multi-Page Support**: Connect multiple brand or sister-agency Facebook Pages to a single CRM account.
- **Campaign Attribution**: View exact Ad Name, Adset ID, Campaign ID, and UTM parameters for every traveler lead.
