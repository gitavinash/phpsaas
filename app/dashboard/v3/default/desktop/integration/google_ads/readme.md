# Google Ads & Lead Extensions Integration

## Overview
Bridge your Google Ads Search, Performance Max, and Display campaigns to capture traveler inquiries directly from Google Lead Forms and sync offline conversion milestones back to Google Ads via GCLID.

## Technical Specifications
- **Plugin Identifier**: `google_ads`
- **Category**: Lead Generation & Advertising
- **Webhook Listener**: `https://yourdomain.com/webhook/google_ads/`

## Features
- **Lead Extension Webhook**: Receive inquiries instantly without travelers needing to land on your website.
- **Offline Conversion Uploads**: Attribute closed high-value bookings back to specific Google Ads keywords and ads.
- **Real-Time Webhook Signing**: Validates incoming Google requests using custom Webhook Key authentication.
