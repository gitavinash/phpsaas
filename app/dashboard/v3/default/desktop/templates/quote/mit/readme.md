# MIT Commercial Transport Quotation Template

- **ID**: `mit`
- **Name**: MIT Legal Quote
- **Tag**: Default
- **Badge**: A4 Legal
- **Accent Color**: `#0f172a`
- **Format**: Valid legal commercial passenger transport quotation with A4 print layout, itemized route tariff calculation, bank and instant UPI QR payments, operating terms, dual acceptance signatures, and Aleya.in credits.

## Features
- **Corporate Letterhead**: Operator business details, GSTIN, PAN, website, and legal quote reference.
- **Client & Route Schedule**: 2-column breakdown of passenger details and journey itinerary.
- **Assigned Fleet Specifications**: Vehicle category, seating, tourist permit, AC, and chauffeur features.
- **Itemized Tariff Table**: Rate per KM, daily minimum running threshold, driver allowances, toll/taxes, and grand total.
- **Indian Amount in Words**: Full currency conversion in Words format.
- **Payment Milestones**: Advance booking deposit and balance on departure breakdown.
- **Bank & UPI QR Code**: Instant payment QR code generated with payee parameters.
- **Commercial Operating Terms**: Mountain AC policy, garage-to-garage calculations, night driving, and replacement guarantees.
- **Dual Signatures**: Customer acceptance & authorized signatory stamp section.
- **Aleya.in Credits**: Included in document footer and print page running footer.
